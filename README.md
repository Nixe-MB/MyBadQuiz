# MyBadQuiz - Exam Reviewer v1.3
A new collection of quizzes!

This website is for educational purposes only.
Please use this website for your reviewer only.

## Features
Subject cards · quiz mode with optional time limit · hints · instant right/wrong feedback (toggle) · shuffle questions/choices ·
score ring + answer review · retry / retry wrong only · saved history (localStorage) · practice mode · light/dark theme ·
keyboard shortcuts (1–4 answer, H hint, ←/→ navigate).

## New in v1.2
Sound effects (synthesized, mute button in header) · confetti and particle bursts · 3D tilt + glow on subject cards · animated aurora background ·
Mega Mix (all subjects) · Flashcards tab · Library tab (search, star/save questions, practice saved) · pause button for timed quizzes ·
auto-next option · XP, levels, day streak and badges · export/import backup · streak flames in practice.
Shortcuts: 1–4 or A–D answer, H hint, S save, P pause, ←/→ navigate, Space flips flashcards.

## New in v1.3
**Arena tab:** Boss Battle (hearts, boss HP, 30 s per-question timer, hit/hurt animations) · Daily Challenge (same 10 questions for everyone each day) ·
Survival (3 lives, endless) · Weak Spots (smart review of questions you miss).
**Mastery map:** every question is a colored square (unseen / weak / learning / mastered) in the History tab.
**Also:** 50/50 lifelines · Ctrl+K (or /) command palette · Settings (4 color palettes, effects on/off, volume, text size, reset) ·
downloadable PNG score card · animated circuit-board background · printable Library (Ctrl+P) · 4 more badges.
Add a boss to a subject with `boss: { name: "...", icon: "👾" }` in its data file (optional).

## New in v1.4
Adds a new logo and favicon, an About page that explains it is for educational use and review only, and two new subject cards, DDC (Data and Digital Communications) and FMSS (Fundamentals of Mixed Signals and Sensors), with 50 handout-based questions each. Subject codes now show as CSA, LCD, DDC and FMSS instead of the IT course numbers, and I rebalanced where the correct answers sit in every question bank. The Boss Battle is now a full quiz shooter in the Arena, with falling answer pods, combos, boss phases, an ultimate, freeze and 50/50 power-ups, three difficulty levels, and procedural battle music and sound effects. I also added an Online Race mode, where friends on other devices join a room code and race through the same questions, a redesigned score-card PNG with a grade, rank and your stats, and 29 badges, up from 11. History gained a score-trend chart, a study heatmap, per-subject stats and clickable mastery squares, and the Library gained a mastery filter. Flashcards can now be limited to weak cards, and there is a daily goal and an accuracy stat on the home page. Settings now include a display name, a music toggle, text size and four color palettes. I fixed the animations and effects not showing on PCs and laptops, which was caused by the site following the system’s reduce-motion setting, and fixed several smaller bugs.
